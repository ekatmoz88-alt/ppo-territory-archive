PPO Territory

Enterprise Information System

PPO is an automated software workstation designed to search for and retrieve information from information resources using specified criteria. The software suite is intended for preparing documents with machine-readable lines and entering their data into the relevant information resources. PPO supports the preparation of documents identical to the provided sample.
